F08  member chain

A member chain that does not start at the receiver may be one deep. A chain
that starts at the receiver may be two deep. Law of Demeter.

Counting
Each `.` or `->` through a member adds one hop. A name on the language
allow-list adds none. A trailing run of calls that ends in `build` adds none.
The finding is the deepest chain in the function, at the line where it starts.

Threshold
Gate at 2 hops from the receiver, and at 1 hop otherwise. No warn level.
The depths are shipped with the profile. A project adds allow-list names and
cannot move the depths.

Before
fn city(response: &Response) -> &str {
    response.body().json().get("city").as_str()
}

After
fn city(response: &Response) -> &str {
    let body = response.body();
    let json = body.json();
    json.get("city").as_str()
}

Anti-fixes
Saving the chain in locals leaves the finding in place.
Splitting the chain across two functions leaves a finding in the second.
A comment on the line does not change the hop count.
