F08  member chain

A member chain that does not start at the receiver may be one deep.
A chain that starts at the receiver may be two deep. Law of Demeter.

Counting
Each `.` or `->` through a member adds one hop. A name on the language
allow-list adds none. A trailing run of calls that ends in `build`
adds none. The finding is the chain that crossed its depth, at the
line where that chain starts.

Threshold
Shipped stand-in: gate at 2 hops from the receiver, and at 1
otherwise, in Rust and C++. Python gates at 3 hops from the
receiver and at 3 otherwise. No warn level. A project adds
allow-list names and cannot move the depths.
A contiguous `# slopmeter: waive <RULE> <NUMBER> <REASON>` run directly
above the function excuses this gate in Python, and the same tokens
after `//` do so in Rust and C++, with a whole number except on F02,
which may be fractional.

Before
fn city(response: &Response) -> &str {
    response.body().json().get("city").as_str()
}

After
fn city(response: &Response) -> &str {
    let body = response.body();
    let json = body.json();
    let city = json.get("city");
    city.as_str()
}

Anti-fixes
Saving the chain in locals leaves the finding in place.
Splitting the chain across two functions leaves a finding in the second.
A comment on the line does not change the hop count.
